Profile

I’m a Data Science and ML practitioner with 5+ years of experience in helping clients build and manage data-driven products. My background sits at the intersection of data science and technical product management, partnering with cross functional teams and stakeholders to deliver customer data products at scale.

As an M.S. candidate at the Center for Data Science, Courant Institute, New York University, I’m currently building deeper expertise in probabilistic modelling, statistical inference, and modern machine learning. I’m particularly interested in using these tools to understand sophisticated, evolving systems, with a focus on financial time-series and natural language processing.

Before NYU, I worked as a Data and AI Consultant at Accenture London, helping clients in financial-services connect the dots between data and product. My work ranged from analysing millions of records to shaping how data was structured, governed, and exchanged between businesses and customers.

Projects

Augmenting Kaplan-Meier Survival Analysis using Ensemble ML Techniques and Containerized Deployment ↗

Developed a Random Survival Forest and XGBSE based application for customer churn prediction and deployed it through Streamlit. Containerized the app and FastAPI model-serving layer with Docker and Docker Compose for reproducible, multi-service deployment.

ALS Recommender System for MovieLens with PySpark ↗

Spark-based movie recommender system comparing popularity baselines, item/user collaborative filtering, and ALS matrix factorization (explicit and implicit).

Beyond Markowitz: Portfolio Optmization with Random Forests and Light GBM ↗

End-to-end, walk-forward pipeline feeding ML (Random Forest, LightGBM) vs. historical-mean return forecasts into four portfolio optimizers — Equal-Weight, Penalised & Regularised Mean-Variance (Markowitz), and Drawdown-Penalised — across 50 S&P 500 stocks.

Statistical Analysis of Ratings data with Regression and Regularisation ↗

Statistical analysis of professor ratings using Bayesian shrinkage, non-parametric testing, bootstrapping, and Lasso regression—with a focus on rigorous inference under messy and sparse data.
